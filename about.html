How It Began

A home that became a hotel.

Himalayan Haven started as a family home on a quiet street just back from Lakeside. In 2017, after years of welcoming friends and travelers who became friends, we opened the doors more widely.

We didn't want to build just another hotel. We wanted to share the Pokhara we love — the slow mornings, the mountain light, the conversations over coffee — with people who would appreciate them. So we kept it small, kept it personal, and grew it one room at a time.

Today, the Haven has twelve rooms, a café, and a terrace with a view that still stops us in our tracks. But the heart of it hasn't changed: it's still a home first.

We believe a stay should feel like a place, not just a room — and that the best journeys are the ones you don't rush.

Our Philosophy

Slow living, thoughtful hospitality.

We're not here to fill rooms. We're here to give people a reason to slow down — to take the morning in, to notice the mountains, to have a conversation that lasts longer than a coffee.

That philosophy shows up everywhere: in the way we welcome guests, in the food we serve, in the spaces we've designed, and in the rhythm of a day at the Haven. Nothing is rushed, and everything is intentional.

Our Connection to Pokhara

A hotel that belongs here.

Pokhara isn't just our location — it's our identity. Our furniture is made by local carpenters. Our coffee comes from a roaster five minutes away. Our team lives in the neighbourhood.

We support a local school, employ guides from nearby villages, and work with artisans who have been practicing their craft for generations. When you stay with us, you support them too.

Hospitality

Welcomed, not processed.

There's no check-in queue, no script, no rush. When you arrive, you're offered tea. When you need something, you ask — and someone who knows your name helps.

Our team is small, and most of us have been here for years. We've watched guests come back for their second, third, fourth visits. That, more than anything, tells us we're doing something right.
