Welcome to Himalayan Haven

A quieter way to experience Pokhara.

Tucked into the calm of Lakeside, Himalayan Haven is a small boutique hotel and café built around the idea that a stay should feel personal. Warm rooms, thoughtful details, and unhurried mornings — all just minutes from Phewa Lake.

We blend Nepalese hospitality with modern comfort. Natural materials, soft light, and mountain views shape every space, while our café serves locally inspired food and fresh coffee from sunrise to late evening.

Our Story

Rooted in Pokhara. Made for travelers.

Himalayan Haven began with a simple belief — that a stay should feel like a place, not just a room. We built a small hotel that celebrates the landscape, the people, and the quiet rhythm of Lakeside life.

Every detail, from the locally crafted furniture to the morning coffee, is shaped by the idea that hospitality should feel personal and unhurried.
